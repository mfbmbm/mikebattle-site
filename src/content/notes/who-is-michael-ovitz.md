## Quotes

- "That was my first lesson in the business - who you know matters."
- "Nothing in Hollywood is anything until it's something. And the way to show it's something is belief."
- "Hollywood has always been a ruthless business."
- "It's only blarney if you can't make it happen."
- "We weren't in a creative medium, we were in a commercially creative medium."

## Lessons

- **Perception is everything.** Pick a persona you can inhabit without strain. CAA agents were taught to "reach for the club" every day but never use it.
- **Use image.** Armani suits for the staff, bright red CAA script covers across Los Angeles, hired Jaguars to take clients to lunch when they had no money.
- **Belief makes projects happen.** Every film lives and dies on belief in it. Lose one key champion and the house of cards can fall.
- **Have the chat.** When a lawyer sent a cease and desist, Ovitz rang the company and said he'd talk to his friends at the justice department if it wasn't rescinded in two hours. He got his way. "It's only blarney if you can't make it happen."
- **Know the terrain.** He watched every Oscar-winning movie in the top five categories to talk craft with directors and writers, and studied Japanese culture before flying to Tokyo for Sony.
- **Be a chameleon.** "If Paul Newman liked clams I liked clams."
- **Remember it's a business.** Creative work still has people counting on it to make money.
- **Honesty cuts through the fluff.** You have to risk alienating clients to do what's best for them. He told Sean Connery he was taking bad project after bad project after Bond. His next film was a hit.
- **Negotiate with the end in mind.** Instil the negative in advance. Plan the desired end point. Buyer and seller remorse is a fact of life.
- **Communicate.** The four CAA commandments: never lie to clients or colleagues, return every call by the end of the day, follow up so no one is left guessing, don't bad-mouth the competition.
- **Build a culture.** Everyone at CAA was a partner, from the mail room up. But in a big company you can start to believe you are the company.
- **Know where the power lies.** "Directors had stars in their pockets." Ask younger people what the next big thing is.
- **In meetings, think before you answer.** "I'll look into that" beats diving in. Make everyone feel comfortable and important.
- **Pitch with motion and emotion.** Use stories and mixed media, understate, and remember "Mystique is 10 times better than publicity."
- **Who you know matters, but the chits add up.** CAA spent $500,000 a year on gifts and Ovitz wrote around 1,000 letters a year. Don't owe favours to too many people.
- **Hollywood is zero-sum.** Everyone is fighting over the same pile of money. Know the world you're working in.
- **Time the market.** He got out of film when TV began to overtake it.
- **Quick tips.** Be a lifetime learner. Be indispensable. Be the outlier. Love what you do. Turn weaknesses into strengths: he signed Dustin Hoffman by pitching his small client list as more time for him. Push boundaries: he fought to get *Shogun* made.
