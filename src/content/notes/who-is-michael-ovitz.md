- 'Nothing in Hollywood is anything until it's something, and the only way to make it something is with a profound display of belief'
- Much of the entertainment business revolves around ego and fear.
- 'Hollywood has always been a ruthless business'
- 'It's only blarney if you can't make it happen, and if you can, it's the truth, and the truth is the supreme sales tool'
- Hollywood is a 'zero-sum business'.
- Perception is everything.
- 'Insecurity and ambition made a powerful cocktail'
- 'That was my first lesson in the business - who you know matters'
- A basic rule for success: 'love what you do'.
- On writers: 'I had a special respect for those that created works from thin air' (Bloody right)
- He planted 'used-looking' scripts with red covers and a white CAA logo all around Hollywood for brand awareness.
- Know the terrain. Read.
- 'We built our company around positive thinking'
- 'We weren't in a creative medium, we were in a commercially creative medium'
- 'If Paul Newman liked clams, I liked clams'
- Be careful with rough cuts. It's hard to envision the final film. When Spielberg showed a rough cut of *Jaws*, the studio said they wouldn't screen it.
- He got into art which proved a good insight into filmmaking. Gave him a basis in visuals. Each film is a bunch of frames.
- Mystique is 10x better than publicity.
- 'He had passed my first test, he was determined'
- 'After 35 you don't know what's going on in culture like the kids do'. He often got kids to tell their influential parents who was the next big thing.
- 'Creative people are much better at taking candid criticism than business people'
- 'In any multiplayer contest you want to be the outlier'
- Pitch with 'motion and emotion'.
- 'No one has a monopoly on good ideas'
- 'That's the tragedy that can befall the company man. We come to believe we are the company'
- 'Operating out of fear is bad business'
